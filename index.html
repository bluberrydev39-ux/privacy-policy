Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how our VR application (“the App”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use it on Meta Quest devices. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Automatically Collected Data (Unity)

Unity may automatically collect non-personal technical information, including device model, operating system, crash logs, and performance metrics. This data helps improve stability and performance.

2.2 Account & Gameplay Data (PlayFab)

If you use online features, PlayFab may collect a player ID, display name, gameplay statistics (scores, progress), session logs, and error reports. PlayFab does not collect real-world personal information unless you explicitly provide it.

2.3 No Sensitive Data

The App does not collect real names, email addresses, phone numbers, physical location, payment information, or biometric data.

3. How We Use Your Data

Data is used only to enable online features, save gameplay progress, prevent cheating, improve performance, fix bugs and crashes, and maintain server stability. We do not sell or share data with advertisers.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following services:

  • PlayFab (Microsoft) – player accounts, cloud saves, leaderboards, multiplayer data.
  • Unity Technologies – engine functionality, crash reporting, performance analytics.

These services may process data on their own servers according to their privacy policies:
PlayFab / Microsoft: https://privacy.microsoft.com/
Unity: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

5. Data Storage & Security

PlayFab stores gameplay data securely on Microsoft Azure servers. Unity stores crash logs and analytics securely on their servers. We do not store data on our own servers. Data is encrypted in transit.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
